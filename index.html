DUTANGA INGEMWE NZIZA KANDI ZIZEWE

Turi abambere mu Rwanda mu gutegura ingemwe kinyamwuga kuva kera cyane. Dutanga ingemwe zifite ubuziranenge buhagije.

TWEMEWE NA RAB

Kuva muri 2005 twahawe icyemezo n'ikigo cy'igihugu gishinzwe ubuhinzi n'ubworozi RAB nyuma yo kwitegereza ubunyamwuga bwacu.

UBUHANGA MU GUKORA UBUSITANI

Dukora ubusitani bujyanye n'aho ushaka kubukorera. Mu ngo, amahoteli, ibigo n'ahandi, ku giciro gito kandi kinyamwuga.

KUTUGERAHO BIROROSHYE

Usibye i Musha ku kicaro gikuru, dufite n'andi mashami menshi mu gihugu. Wadusanga kandi ku mbuga nkoranyambaga.

AHANTU HEZA HO KURUHUKIRA

Eden Garden iguha ahantu heza ho kuruhukira no kwishimira. Mwe banyamahanga namwe bene gihugu mugana i Burasirazuba cyangwa muvayo, munyure muri Eden Garden.

AMAFUNGURO YIHARIYE

Amafunguro n'ibinyobwa biteguye neza. Umwihariko wacu ni uko tubafitiye n'indyo za Kinyarwanda ku giciro cyiza kandi mu isuku ihagije.

IMYIDAGADURO

Igihe cyiza cyo kwishimana n'inshuti n'umuryango. Ngwino hano maze tubahe icyo kurya, kunywa, mwidagaduze mukina biliyari munarebera imikino itandukanye.

AMACUMBI MEZA

Aho kuruhukira mu isuku ihagije n'umutekano. Waba uri umwe cyangwa muri benshi, mushobora kuza mukarara iwacu ku biciro byoroheje.

PARIKINGI IHAGIJE

Parking itekanye kandi ihagije ku bashyitsi bacu. Ushaka guparika umwanya muto cyangwa munini? Ntuhangayike kuko umutekano wacu urihariye.
