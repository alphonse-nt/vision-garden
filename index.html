Kuki Wahitamo Vision Garden?

🌱 Ingemwe nziza

Muri Vision Garden dutanga ingemwe nziza kandi zatoranyijwe neza nkuko bigenwa na RAB.

🚚 Kugusanga aho uri

Dushobora kukugezaho ibicuruzwa byacu aho uri hose mu Rwanda. Vision Garden ifite amashami muri Gasabo, Ruhango, Nyagatare na Musanze, bityo tukaba dushobora kugufasha kubona ingemwe nziza hafi yawe.

Tugufasha kumenya uko watera kandi ukita ku biti. Dufite aba agronome bakugira inama zuko wategura neza ubutaka,ibiti biberanye n'ubutaka,uko byitabwaho,...
