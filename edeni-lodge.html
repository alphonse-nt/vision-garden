Twongeye kubaha ikaze ku macumbi ya eden-garden

Eden Garden ibateguriye amacumbi meza, ahantu hatuje kandi heza ho kuruhukira nyuma y'umunsi mwiza.

Twakira abagenzi, imiryango ndetse n'abashaka ahantu ho kuruhukira mu mutuzo n'umutekano.

✨ Ibyo tubafitiye

🛏️ Ibyumba bifite isuku

Ibyumba biteguye neza kandi bigahora bisukuye kugira ngo mugire ijoro ryiza.

🌿 Ahantu hatuje

Ushobora kuruhukira ahantu hatuje kure y'umuvuduko w'ubuzima bwa buri munsi.

🍽️ Serivisi zunganira

Ushobora kubona amafunguro n'ibinyobwa igihe ubikeneye.

🤝 Kwakira neza

Abashyitsi bacu tubakira neza kandi tugaharanira ko bagira uburambe bwiza.

📞 Baza amakuru
