Tubafitiye Billard ibafasha kwidagadura, kuruhuka no gusabana n'inshuti.

Eden Garden ni ahantu hihariye hateguwe kugira ngo abantu bose bishime, baruhuke kandi bagirane ibihe byiza. Niba ukunda umukino wa Billard, aha ni ho hantu heza ho kuwukina mu mwuka w'ibyishimo n'ubusabane.

Waza wenyine, hamwe n'inshuti, umuryango cyangwa abo mukorana. Turabakira neza kandi tugaharanira ko buri wese asubira mu rugo afite ibyishimo n'urwibutso rwiza.
