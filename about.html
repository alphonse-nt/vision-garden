Vision Garden 🌱

Pepiniyeri y'ibiti byiza mu Rwanda

Abo Turibo

Vision Garden ni pepiniyeri yemewe na RAB (Rwanda Agriculture and Animal Resources Development Board), ikora ibikorwa byo gutunganya no kugurisha amoko atandukanye y'ibiti.

Yatangiye ibikorwa byayo mu mwaka wa 2005, igamije gufasha abantu kubona ingemwe nziza z'ibiti by'imbuto, ibiti by'amashyamba, indabo n'ibindi biti byo kurimbisha no kurengera ibidukikije.

Vision Garden iherereye mu Murenge wa Musha, Akarere ka Rwamagana, ariko ifite n'andi mashami hirya no hino mu gihugu kugira ngo yegere abakiriya bayo.

Umuyobozi Mukuru (CEO)

Sibomana Emmanuel ni Umuyobozi Mukuru wa Vision Garden. Ayobora ibikorwa byo guteza imbere pepiniyeri no gutanga ingemwe nziza ku bakiriya.

Intego Yacu

Intego yacu ni ugutanga ingemwe zifite ubuziranenge, gufasha abahinzi n'abakiriya kubona ibisubizo birambye, no guteza imbere ubuhinzi n'ibidukikije mu Rwanda.
