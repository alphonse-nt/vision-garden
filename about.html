📞 Twandikire

🌿 Vision Garden Company Ltd

Vision Garden Company Ltd ni ikigo gikora ibikorwa bijyanye n'ingemwe, ibiti, ubusitani, kurengera ibidukikije ndetse na serivisi za Eden Garden. Duharanira gufasha abakiriya kubona ibiti n'ingemwe nziza kandi tubafasha no kumenya uko babyitaho.

📖 AMATEKA YACU

🌱 Twatangiye mu 2005

Vision Garden yatangiye ibikorwa byayo mu mwaka wa 2005, ifite intego yo gufasha abantu kubona ingemwe nziza no guteza imbere gutera ibiti mu Rwanda.

🌳 Ubuziranenge

Dushyira imbere ubuziranenge bw'ingemwe n'ibiti dutanga, kugira ngo abakiriya babone ibisubizo birambye.

✅ Twemewe na RAB

Vision Garden ifite uburambe mu bikorwa bya pepiniyeri kandi yemewe n'inzego zibishinzwe.

📍 AHO DUHEREREYE

Vision Garden iherereye mu Murenge wa Musha, Akarere ka Rwamagana. Dukomeza kwegera abakiriya bacu binyuze mu mashami n'imikoranire ahantu hatandukanye mu Rwanda.

🌿 GUTEZA IMBERE UBUSITANI

Gufasha abantu, ibigo n'amahoteli kubaka ubusitani bwiza kandi bujyanye n'aho bashaka kubukorera.

🌳 KURENGERA IBIDUKIKIJE

Dushishikariza gutera ibiti, kurengera ibidukikije no kugira uruhare mu Rwanda rutoshye kandi rwiza.

👨‍🌾 UBUJYANAMA

Dufasha abakiriya guhitamo ibiti bikwiye, kubitera no kubyitaho bitewe n'aho batuye n'intego bafite.

🏡 EDEN GARDEN

Mu muryango wa Vision Garden harimo na Eden Garden, ahantu ho kuruhukira, gusangira, kwidagadura no gucumbika.

🤝 KWAKIRA ABAKIRIYA NEZA
